# Contributing

Contributions that improve the clarity, reproducibility, or correctness of the road-safety analyses are welcome.

## Guidelines

- Open an issue before introducing a major analytical or methodological change.
- Describe data source, time/geographic coverage, preprocessing, assumptions, and limitations.
- Keep notebooks readable and reproducible; avoid committing large raw datasets or generated outputs.
- Do not present correlations as causal effects or add predictive claims without a documented evaluation.

## Pull requests

Use a focused branch and explain the change, the notebook(s) affected, and the validation performed. When relevant, include the cells or commands used to reproduce changed results. Check that notebooks contain no private information, local absolute paths, or unnecessary output before submitting.
